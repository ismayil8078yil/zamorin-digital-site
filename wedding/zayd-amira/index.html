Our Love Story

Two lives, one quiet certainty

They met in the reading room of a quiet Kochi library — two strangers reaching for the same worn copy of poetry. What began as a shared umbrella in the monsoon became a shared language: long walks, longer letters, and a calm, unshakeable certainty.

Nine years, four cities and countless cups of chai later, Zayd asked the question beneath the very rain-tree where they first took shelter. Amira said yes before he finished the sentence. Now, beneath the white sails of Kochi's harbour, they begin forever.

— Z & A
